# SchoolPop — In a Nutshell

**by Mhortai**

SchoolPop makes World of Warcraft 3.3.5a combat easier to read. It replaces the game's plain floating damage numbers with ones **colour-coded by damage school**, and adds your own private **combat log** and a per-fight **damage/healing breakdown**.

## What you get

- **Colour-coded floating numbers.** Every hit floats up in its school's colour — Physical, Holy, Fire, Nature, Frost, Shadow, Arcane, and Healing — so you know the *type* of damage at a glance. Crits are bigger with `!!!`. Big numbers shorten to K / M / B / T.
- **Your own combat log.** A clean window showing only *your* damage and healing, one line each, coloured by school — with a live **DPS / HPS meter** at the bottom.
- **A DPS/HPS breakdown.** Two tabs (DPS and HPS) listing every ability and proc you used this fight, with its total, share %, hits and per-second rate. Each proc is listed separately by its spell ID.
- **Fully customisable.** Change colours, fonts, size, direction (including an arc), and more — all from an in-game panel.

## Getting started in 30 seconds

1. Put the `SchoolPop` folder in `Interface\AddOns\` and restart the game.
2. Click the **gem on your minimap**, or type **`/sp`**, to open the options.
3. Tick **Log window** for the combat log, or click **Breakdown** for the per-fight breakdown.
4. That's it — go hit something.

Everything applies instantly; there's nothing to configure to get started. For the full details, see the complete How-To guide.
